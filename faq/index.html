Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about paying energy, electricity, gas, and utility bills.

☎ Call 1-800-PAY-BILLS✉ Email Us

What energy and utility providers do you support?

We support bill payment services for hundreds of energy utilities across the United States, including major providers such as PG&E, Duke Energy, Southern California Edison, Consumers Energy, Dominion Energy, FPL, DTE Energy, Xcel Energy, Georgia Power, Con Edison, and many more.

How do I pay my energy bill online?

Call 1-800-PAY-BILLS or email info@energy-bill-pay.com. Have your utility provider, account details, and payment amount ready.

Is it safe to pay my utility bill through your service?

Payment information may be handled by third-party payment processors. Review the applicable payment and privacy information before completing a transaction.

How quickly will my energy payment be processed?

Payments are processed promptly, but the time for a payment to post to a utility account can vary by utility company and payment method.

What information do I need to make a payment?

Have your utility provider name, account number, service address, and payment amount ready.

Can I pay my gas bill and electric bill together?

Contact our customer service team with each utility account and payment amount to discuss available options.

Do you charge any fees for bill payment services?

Service fees may apply. Any applicable fees should be disclosed before a transaction is completed.

What if I made a mistake with my payment?

Contact us as soon as possible at 1-800-PAY-BILLS or info@energy-bill-pay.com so the transaction can be reviewed.

Can I set up automatic recurring payments?

Contact our customer service team at 1-800-PAY-BILLS for information about available recurring payment options.

What are your customer service hours?

Our website states customer service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at 1-800-PAY-BILLS.

Will I receive a receipt for my payment?

A payment confirmation is provided after a transaction is processed.

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods can vary. Contact 1-800-PAY-BILLS for current options.
